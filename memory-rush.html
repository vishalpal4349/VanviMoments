How to Play

Master the celebration symbols with pure focus and speed.

🌸 1. Preview & Memorize: All celebration cards are revealed for 2–3 seconds. Memorize the symbol pairs!
🔄 2. Flip & Match: Tap 2 cards. If symbols match, they stay unlocked with glowing green aura.
⚠️ 3. Mistakes & Penalty: Wrong guesses cost 1 mistake and -2 seconds. Keep an eye on the countdown clock!
🪙 4. Tiered Rewards: Easy mode awards starter coins (+2 Coins), while Hard and Expert award massive coins (+8 to +12 Coins)!
🛡️ 5. Daily Limit: Safe 30 Coins daily cap across all celebration games.
