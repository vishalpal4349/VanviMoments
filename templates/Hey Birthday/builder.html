the letter

The final message ✉️

This is the big one — the letter shown at the very end, right after the candles are blown out. Don't overthink it; write like you're talking to them.

Tips for a great message
  • Skip the formal “Dear…” — jump right in like you're talking to them
  • Say why today matters, early on
  • Add one specific detail — a memory, an inside joke, something you admire
  • A little humor keeps it feeling like you, not a store card
  • End on a warm, sincere wish
