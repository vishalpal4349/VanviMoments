## 🌟 Interactive Journey (6 Chapters)

1. **Chapter 0: Magazine Cover** — Dynamic recipient name, tilted badges, sweet opening note, animated stickers.
2. **Chapter 1: Tic-Tac-Love Game** — Playable mini-game where placing the missing heart completes 3-in-a-row and triggers celebratory confetti!
3. **Chapter 2: Wax-Sealed Apology Letter** — Vintage postcard design with wax seal badge, personalized cursive signature, and heartfelt apology note.
4. **Chapter 3: Polaroid Photo Memories** — 3 tactile polaroids with handwritten captions, rotation angles, and smooth photo navigation.
5. **Chapter 4: Romantic Mini Vinyl Jukebox** — Real Web Audio player with working seekbar, animated equalizer bars, and track switcher.
6. **Chapter 5: Climax & Hug Finale** — Animated hug sticker, confetti explosion, "Claim a Hug" interaction, and 1-Click WhatsApp share.
