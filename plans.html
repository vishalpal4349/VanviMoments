Frequently Asked Questions

❓ What is the difference between Memberships and Celebration Passes?
VIP Memberships provide continuous access to the entire template catalog, unlimited AI tools, and monthly cloud storage. Celebration Passes are 1-time prepaid bundles (320, 700, or 1,500 credits) with zero recurring subscriptions; credits never expire for 6 months and can be redeemed on any template.
❓ How do Vanvi Coins work at checkout?
10 Coins equal ₹1.00 store credit. On budget/starter creations (up to ₹299), creators can redeem up to 60% of the price with coins, while mid-tier templates support up to 50%, and luxury high-ticket templates support up to 40% (capped at ₹500 discount for security). Unspent coins never expire.
❓ Can I cancel my Vanvi Club subscription anytime?
Yes, you can cancel at any time directly from your Creator Dashboard. Your Club benefits remain active until the end of your billing cycle.
