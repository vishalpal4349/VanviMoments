Questions

Is it really 100% free?

Yes. You can make and download as many high-resolution invitation cards as you like for both WhatsApp and printing.

Which size should I download?

Choose Post (1080 × 1350) for WhatsApp direct chat messages and Instagram grid posts. Choose Story (9:16) for WhatsApp Status and Instagram stories.

How does the Golden QR code work?

When you enter your event venue's Google Maps link, a crisp QR code is automatically generated on your invitation card. When guests scan it with their phone camera, Google Maps opens instantly for turn-by-turn navigation.

Can I use my Cloudinary image?

Yes! Simply paste your Cloudinary link into the photo field and click "Load". It renders instantly inside the card's royal frame.
